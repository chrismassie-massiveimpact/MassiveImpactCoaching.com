Common Questions

Straight Answers

How long does setup take? +
Most bots are live within 5–7 business days. We handle everything — we just need your services, pricing, and service area. You'll approve the bot before it goes live.
Do I need a website already? +
No. We can build you one, or host your bot on a standalone page. If you already have a website, we embed the bot directly onto your existing pages.
What if I want to change my pricing or services? +
Easy. We update your bot whenever you need. Minor changes (pricing, hours, service area) are included in your monthly plan. Major rebuilds are quoted separately.
Is there a contract? +
No long-term contracts. Monthly plans are month-to-month. We keep clients because the bot works — not because of a contract.
What industries do you serve? +
We currently serve lawn care, landscaping, and snow removal companies. Home services, real estate, and office scheduling are coming soon. Each industry gets a bot built specifically for their workflow — not a generic template.
